<Eyebrow>Key project</Eyebrow>

# A slow response process was eroding trust

A student support team handled enquiries across three disconnected queues.

**The project question:** How might we respond faster without sacrificing the quality of advice?

<div class="syd-data-note"><strong class="text-ochre">Role ·</strong> Process mapping, design facilitation, and workflow testing.</div>
